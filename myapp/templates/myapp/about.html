About ByteStream

Engineering ideas, made clear.

ByteStream is an independent engineering publication built to make complex technology understandable and useful. We go beyond the headlines to explore how systems work, why design choices matter, and what engineers can learn from the people building the future.

Our mission is to share rigorous, thoughtful writing that helps curious readers deepen their craft and make better-informed decisions.

What guides us

Our core values

01 / Understand

Technical depth

We look beneath the surface, bringing context, clear explanations, and respect for the details that make technology work.

02 / Share

Open knowledge

Useful engineering knowledge should be accessible. We welcome diverse perspectives and make room for ideas to be shared openly.

03 / Improve

Continuous growth

Technology keeps changing, and so do we. We stay curious, learn from experience, and keep improving our understanding.
